## BgGone

**BgGone (bgg.one)** handles images in the browser. Two things are ready: remove a background, and compress images. The files stay on this device. No login, and nothing is uploaded.

## Remove a background

Take the background off in one step, including a batch, and download a transparent PNG. The original quality stays, with no watermark.

Drag, paste, or choose the images. The work runs in this browser.

[Remove a background](/)

## Compress images

Shrink a batch of JPEG, PNG, and WebP files, or turn an iPhone HEIC into a JPEG. Choose a quality, limit the long edge, or aim for a file size, then save the files one by one or as one ZIP.

[Compress images](/compress/)
